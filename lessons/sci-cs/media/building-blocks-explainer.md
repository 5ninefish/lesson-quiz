## Narration

Every program is built from a few simple building blocks. Let's find them in everyday life.

First, sequence. Shoes on, then walk out, then done.

A sequence is actions in a specific order.

Swap two steps, and you're out the door without your shoes. A list in the wrong order is still a sequence. It's just not the one you wanted.

Next, a loop. Stir, stir, stir, until the sauce is thick.

The stirring is the repeated body. And "until thick" is the stopping condition.

Now, a conditional. If it's raining, take an umbrella. Otherwise, leave it.

The rain is the condition. Taking the umbrella, or leaving it, is the action.

Finally, a program: instructions expressed so a machine can run them.

A recipe on paper is an algorithm. That same recipe, written so a machine can follow it, is a program.

Sequence, loop, conditional, program. Look for them in the instructions around you.
